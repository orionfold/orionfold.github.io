**Your personal Fold gives the way you work a place of its own.** Put standing instructions in your Fold’s files, or use **Remember** in Flow Pro to keep a preference from Chat. Flow can bring relevant saved memories into later work. Teach it once, and your Fold keeps it.

> “Every brief starts with what we’re deciding. I’d like to stop explaining that before every draft.”
> *Illustrative scenario.*

## Give Flow one preference worth keeping

1. Open **Folds** (⌘6). Your personal Fold’s files are listed with its tools. **USER** opens **About you**. In our sample we edited and saved that page directly.
2. In Flow Pro, open Chat with your personal Fold and start the message with **Remember**. Try “Remember that weekly delivery briefs should put the decision in the first sentence.”
3. Use **Show** to inspect what Flow saved, or **Undo** to take it back. On a configured overnight learning run, look in **Morning Briefing** for what your Fold learned and the Undo control.

A saved preference works best when it is specific enough to use in many pieces of work. “Put the decision in the first sentence” gives a Fold something concrete to carry into a future brief. Flow selects memories relevant to the run and can fetch a listed memory if it needs the detail.
